PROJECT UPDATE: 2026-06-07 - Stoichiometry Engine & Architectural Patterns

8. Stoichiometry Implementation:
- Started `engine/stoichiometry.py`.

9. Architectural Patterns Introduced:
- Atomic Functions: Breaking complex calculations into the smallest possible reusable pieces.
    - Intra-substance conversion: `mass_to_moles`, `moles_to_mass` (Unit changes).
    - Inter-substance conversion: `mole_ratio_conversion` (Substance changes).
- Pipeline Architecture: Chaining atomic functions to solve complex problems (e.g., `mass_mass_calculation`).
- Dimensional Analysis Mapping: Aligned programmatic "Step-by-Step Pipeline" with chemical "Chain of Fractions" on paper.

10. Key Learning Milestones:
